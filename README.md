## The project

Arthur is making his way onto the **Neo Geo Pocket Color**. The aim is to retain the challenge and recognizable stages of the Amiga *Ghosts 'n Goblins* conversion while adding NGPC-friendly presentation, quality-of-life features and a rather unexpected detour into the SNK world.

There are **two visual/story paths**: the familiar **Classic** game and the optional **SNK World** route. SNK World isn't simply a palette toggle: there is new character art, dialogue, crossover sequences and a different ending path. The underlying stages remain rooted in *Ghosts 'n Goblins*.

The latest private development build represented here is **M51R1 (29 October 2026)**. These are real screenshots captured from that build, not concept art or a trailer mock-up.

## Beyond the original adventure

- **Seven stages** adapted for handheld play, including the original enemies and bosses.
- **SNK story route**, with an introduction, portals, adapted character art and additional dialogue.
- **Guest assistance system** featuring ten crossover characters; guest movement, attacks and stage-key handling are built into the game. A helper returns control to Arthur when a stage key appears.
- **Ranked and Free save options**. Free saving is convenient but disables ranked scoring. In Ranked mode, saved checkpoints are consumed when loaded.
- **Lives and continues settings**, with one to nine continues. Settings above three starting lives or continues make the run unranked.
- **Records and QR-score functionality** built into the game; public leaderboard availability/acceptance is not announced here.
- **A custom SNK-route finale**, with its own playable sequence and post-game scenes (spoilers below).

## Controls and progress

**D-pad** to move and climb, **A** to throw your current weapon, **B** to jump. **Option** opens the pause/menu controls in play. Menu screens show their own prompts.

The normal development ROM is intended for a compatible **Neo Geo Pocket Color emulator or original NGPC hardware**, but it is **not publicly downloadable from this repository yet**. There is no release, install guide or download link to follow at present.

For details on the gameplay rules and what is currently verified, see [Gameplay & status](docs/GAMEPLAY_AND_STATUS.md). For the illustrated draft booklet, [read the M50 instruction manual](docs/Ghosts_n_Goblins_M50_Manual.pdf).

## Current development status

This is an active port, **not a claim that final hardware QA or a release candidate is complete**. Latest local M51R1 tests cover the Terry visibility fix, the enlarged fight-scene Arthur, crossover ending paths and controlled performance checks across all seven stages. Those checks were performed with the **NGPCraft emulator**, including controlled scene fixtures; they do not replace a complete human playthrough or extended testing on real hardware.

**A playable build will be announced separately when ready.** The artwork, instructions and screenshots here are development showcase materials only; no game ROM or manufactured product is available.
